About Us

Our Story

Shree Vighnaharta Ganesh Mandal was founded by a small group of neighbours who wanted the festival to feel like a family gathering rather than a formal event. What began as a modest idol in a courtyard has grown into a ten-day celebration that brings families together.

Every year we host aarti evenings, cultural programs for children, a community bhandara, and an eco-friendly visarjan procession — all run entirely by volunteers and funded by the generosity of our neighbourhood.
